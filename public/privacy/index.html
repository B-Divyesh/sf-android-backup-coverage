Privacy

Last updated: August 27, 2026.

Your files stay with you

Folder scans, file names, sizes, modified dates, manifests, settings, and verification receipts are processed and stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. Android Backup Coverage does not upload or receive them. Export happens only when you choose it.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, checkout and license verification are handled by Sociobot and Dodo, the merchant of record. This app stores your license token and a cached validity result in local browser storage. The token is sent only to the Sociobot license verification endpoint.

Network use

The installed app may request its own files for updates. It contains no advertising, behavioral analytics, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

Control

Use “Erase local data” in the footer to remove manifests and receipts. Browser site-data controls can remove all saved data, including the license token. Exported files remain wherever you saved them.

Contact

Questions can be sent through sociobot.in.
