What a backup check means

A match means the relative path and byte size were equal when checked. It does not prove file contents or future storage health.

What the app does not do

The app compares folders or file lists that you choose. It does not create a backup or guarantee recovery.

Your responsibility

Use only folders and file lists you may inspect. Keep independent copies and test important restores before deleting files.

Price and access

This release is free and has no paid tier. All checks, saved history, reminders, and exports are available without an account.

Availability and warranty

The app is provided “as is” without warranties. To the extent allowed by law, its authors are not liable for lost files or decisions based on a check.
