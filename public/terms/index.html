What the app does

Android Backup Coverage compares user-selected, browser-readable file metadata. It does not upload files, create backups, read app-private data, guarantee storage durability, or replace testing your restore process.

Your responsibility

Keep at least two independent copies and verify important restores. A “verified” result means a destination file matched the source relative path and size at check time; it is not a cryptographic content guarantee.
